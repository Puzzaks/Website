# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** March 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the website (`puzzak.page` and its subdomains) and all Android applications published by Puzzak on Google Play or GitHub.

As an individual developer, I believe in transparency and privacy. All of my apps and this website are open-source, meaning anyone can review the source code to verify exactly how data is handled.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

I do not collect, store, or share any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) or sensitive personal data. I do not track individual users, nor do I collect unique device identifiers or user-generated content (such as conversations or inputted text).

### 1.1 Website (`puzzak.page`)
The website uses Google Analytics to collect general, aggregated analytical data (e.g., page views, visitor country statistics). 
- The website itself does not ask for or collect any user input.
- The website does not set any cookies other than those strictly required by Google Analytics for basic statistical tracking.
- The Link Shortener service (`link.puzzak.page`) only counts the number of times a link has been followed and does not store any personal data by itself.

### 1.2 Android Applications
My applications use Google Analytics for Firebase to collect basic, aggregated usage statistics. This helps me understand which features are being used, device models (e.g., how many Pixel 9s vs. Pixel 10s are running the app), general regions of my users, and app load stages.
- **Opt-Out:** Users have a toggle within the apps to completely disable analytics tracking. Once disabled, it remains off unless you explicitly turn it back on.
- **Crash Reporting:** In the future, Firebase Crashlytics may be integrated to collect anonymized crash reports to help resolve bugs. Additionally, standard aggregated performance metrics are provided to me by the Google Play Console (such as installation counts and crash rates).
- **App-Specific Contexts:**
  - **Telegram Channel Analyzer:** Requires you to log in to your Telegram account. However, all data processing occurs entirely locally on your device. No data leaves your device to be sent to me or any third-party servers.
  - **PAIOS (Gemini Nano):** Allows you to chat with a local AI model. All conversations are processed locally on your device and are never collected, transmitted, or accessed by me.
  - **Other apps:** If the app does not have toggle to enable or disable analytics in the settings, it means that there is no analytics tracking in the app.

## 2. Data Sharing

I will not give access to my Google Analytics, Firebase, or Google Play Console data to any third party. However, I may occasionally analyze and share strictly aggregated and anonymized statistical data on social media (e.g., "The app has reached 1,000 users!").

Because the data I collect is completely anonymous and aggregated, it cannot be used to track or identify any individual user.

## 3. Data Deletion
Because I do not collect or store any information linked to specific individuals, there is no personal data for me to delete upon request.

## 4. Open Source
Transparency is a core principle of my work. The source code for my website and applications is publicly available on my GitHub repositories. You are encouraged to review the code yourself to verify these privacy practices.
